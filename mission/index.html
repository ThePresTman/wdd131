Mission Statement

Brigham Young University-Idaho

Brigham Young University-Idaho was founded and is supported and guided by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Its mission is to develop disciples of Jesus Christ who are leaders ih their home, the Church, and their communities.

The university does this by:

  1. Building testimonies of the rstored gospel of Jesus Christ and fostering its principles in a wholesome academic, cultural, and sociul environment.
  2. Providing a high-quality education that prepares students of diverse interests and abilities for lifelong learning and employment.
  3. Serving as many students as possible within resource constraints.
  4. Delivering education that is afforable for students and the Church.
